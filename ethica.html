Her mission, in three pillars

The PDR Framework

Every fraud Ethica fights is met the same way — prevent it before it starts, detect it while it's happening, and remediate the damage once it's found.

01

Prevention

Policies, password controls, approved vendor lists, and internal controls put in place before fraud has a chance to occur.

02

Detection

Forensic accounting, data analysis, audit trails, and anomaly detection to spot fraud in progress before losses mount.

03

Remediation

Restoring financial integrity, recovering losses, reporting to the right authorities, and educating victims so it never happens twice.

What she teaches

Practical protections
any team can put in place.

Require dual authorization for all large financial transactions.

Use strong, unique passwords on every accounting system and change them regularly.

Maintain approved vendor lists to block unauthorized payments.

Monitor corporate card statements for personal purchases.

Run surprise audits of point-of-sale systems on a regular cadence.

Reconcile bank statements monthly — without exception.

Separate financial duties so no single person controls a transaction end to end.

Train all staff to recognize phishing and social-engineering attempts.

Report suspicious activity immediately through proper channels.
