Frequently asked questions

Everything you're wondering.

What is a branded digital twin?

A branded digital twin is an interactive AI version of you — built from your image, your cloned voice, and a knowledge base of your expertise. It lives on your website, talks with visitors in real time, answers their questions the way you would, and captures leads for you around the clock.

Will it replace me?

No — and it shouldn't. Your twin amplifies you; it never replaces you. It handles the greetings, the common questions, and the first conversations, so the moments that need the real you get more of you, not less.

Do visitors know they're talking to an AI?

Always. Every conversation is clearly disclosed as an AI digital twin, not a live person. Transparency is built in — which is exactly why audiences trust it.

What do I need to provide?

Yourself, mostly. We guide you through a photo capture session and a voice recording session, then gather your documents, website, and story for the knowledge base. We handle the building; you approve everything before it goes live.

Can it go on the website I already have?

Yes. Your twin can live on its own branded page or be added to your existing website with a single line of code — whichever fits your brand.
