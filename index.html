How does AI digital cloning work?

AvatarAgency captures your likeness and voice in a single session, then uses that clone to produce professional video content across every platform — without you showing up again. We use the latest AI video and voice technologies to generate cinematic footage, sync your cloned voice, and deliver finished videos ready to post.

How much content can one cloning session produce?
