Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

How does AI digital cloning work?

We capture your likeness and voice in a single session, then use that digital twin to produce professional video across every platform — without you showing up again. We generate cinematic footage on the newest AI engines, sync your cloned voice, and hand-finish every video in a professional edit.

Do I have to be on camera all the time?

Once. One guided capture session builds your digital twin. After that, we produce new video of you without you re-shooting anything.

Will it look like “AI content”?

No — and that's the point of having a strategist. Every piece is storyboarded, generated on the best engine for the shot, and hand-finished in a professional edit. If it looks fake, it doesn't ship.

What platforms are the videos made for?

Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube and X — widescreen for your website and YouTube, vertical for social.

What is a talking AI agent?

A lifelike avatar on your website that speaks with visitors face to face, in real time. It answers questions in a natural voice, follows your customer's language, remembers returning visitors, books appointments on your calendar and captures every lead. We build three kinds: a digital twin of you, a custom avatar designed for your brand, or a ready-to-go presenter from our Signature Collection. Meet Ava.

What is AI Referral Optimization?

People now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity and Claude for recommendations, and the AI answers with two or three names. We make your business one of them — starting with a free AI Referral Audit that tests five real customer questions on all five AIs.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is scoped to your market and goals on a free 30-minute strategy call. You leave knowing the number and the plan, with no obligation.

How fast will I see something?

Your first production is typically in your hands within weeks of your capture session — not months. Urgent projects can move faster.
