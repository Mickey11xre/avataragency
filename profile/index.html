01

Executive summary

A creative director's eye. An operator's patience.

Michael Rivera is the founder and chief creative strategist of AvatarAgency.ai, a Los Angeles AI marketing studio that builds digital twins, cinematic AI video, real-time talking AI agents, interactive websites, and AI Referral Optimization for companies that want the presence big brands pay agencies for. He also created LiveBrand.ai, a platform that turns a professional's face, voice and knowledge into an agent that works on their website around the clock.

His career began in 1991 at Activision, as one of its earliest employees during the turnaround that made it a dominant force in gaming. Over eight years he managed quality control, customer support and documentation through launches including MechWarrior 2, Quake II and Interstate '76, as revenue grew from $57.8 million to more than $312 million. He then joined the founding team of Ignited, a Los Angeles integrated marketing agency, where as senior online producer he led writers, designers and photographers on campaigns for Sony, Motorola, Ubisoft, and the U.S. Army.

From 2006 he spent eight years in Costa Rica luxury real estate development — first as vice president of marketing, running integrated digital campaigns and lead generation for international buyers, then as director of residential services, running homeowner relations, property management and the vacation-rental program. Since 2023 he has been executive vice president of business development at Amagaya, involved in land acquisition, modeling sustainable, tech-integrated luxury housing ventures in Costa Rica and Colorado.

That combination is what he brings to a board: the creative judgment to know what will move a market, the operator's patience to see a long, complex project through, and hands-on fluency with today's generative AI. He works as a fractional CMO for owner-led companies and is seeking advisory and board roles where revenue growth, brand and AI adoption are on the agenda.

Michael holds a BFA from the University of Michigan and an AI Assisted Consultant Certification, and is a keynote speaker with THRIVE, the speaking duo he leads with Dr. Stephanie Geter.
