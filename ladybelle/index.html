The storybook series

One little puppy.
Nine big lessons.

Tales of Lady Belle is a Christian children's picture-book series by Adele Harrison. Each book follows Lady Belle through her first year in her new home — one season, one virtue at a time — teaching the fruit of the Spirit from Galatians 5:22-23 through the eyes of a gentle, hopeful little dog.

"Love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control."
— Galatians 5:22-23

The illustrations show the world from the pets' point of view — mostly black and white except for the animal in focus — giving the series a distinctive, intimate visual style that puts children right in the story.

The cast

A puppy, two cats,
and a family learning together.

01

The Star

Lady Belle

A sweet white Snoodle (schnauzer-poodle) puppy, recently adopted into a loving family of four. Hopeful, gentle, curious, and brave-in-progress. Her job is clearing morsels from the floor, and her greatest love is a warm lap and her first Christmas snow.

02

The Schemer

Shadow

All-black, long and lean, with bright yellow eyes and canines that show when his mouth is closed. Shadow is the leader of the two cat brothers — the one who comes up with the teasing. His habit of pouncing gives Belle a good reason to be nervous.

03

The Muscle

Cuddles

Big, fat, and tiger-striped. Cuddles is Shadow's brother and backup — the two work as a team. Both cats were adopted too, and their early hissing (which gave them the name "the Hissers") gave way to a real friendship once they realized they all shared the same family.

Lady Belle's first year

Six books.
One journey home.

Each book follows the seasons of Lady Belle's first year — one virtue woven through each everyday adventure.

Winter

Kindness

Adoption day: the contrast between the family's warm kindness and the cats' hostility. Belle names them the "Hissers" and learns what kindness really looks like — and how to give it even when it isn't given back.

Winter

Self-Control

Lady Belle learns to stop barking at every noise, every shadow, every cat. By the end she is calling Shadow and Cuddles by their real names — proof that self-control is also a kind of courage.

Spring

Patience · Goodness · Peace

Shadow learns not to pounce. The family adds bell collars so Belle hears them coming. The cats reveal they were adopted too and were simply unsure about sharing their family. Peace is made — not forced, but grown.

Spring

Gentleness

Patient house-training, no harsh discipline, the family teaching the youngest child gentle play. Belle receives a sharp "NO" for nipping and learns that gentleness given and received feels the same — like being held carefully.

Summer

Faithfulness

The family vacations. Belle, Shadow, and Cuddles go to "Camp." They miss their family, choose to stay faithful, and discover that love keeps waiting — and that faithfulness looks like loyalty even when no one is watching.

Winter

Love & Joy

December. Belle's first snow. Christmas — the tree, the presents, the big meal, a houseful of laughter. She feels the warmth of her whole family and finally understands what love and joy really mean: this. Right here.
