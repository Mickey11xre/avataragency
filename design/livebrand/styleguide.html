01 · Foundation

Personal brand, always on air.

LiveBrand.ai is the Squarespace for Digital Twins — a platform where professionals deploy interactive AI avatars of themselves. One through-line: live / on-air. A broadcast presence that is always on, always on-brand.

Clean
The Apple spirit

Restrained layouts, generous whitespace, nothing wasted. White is the primary ground.

The Coca-Cola spirit

Human, iconic, emotionally resonant. Warm neutrals and a serif voice keep technology human-first.

The Tesla spirit

Technical, exact, future-facing. One electric accent used sparingly — the on-air signal.

Clear space & minimum size
  • Clear space on all sides = the outer ring diameter (≈1.4× circle).
  • Minimum lockup height: 24px digital, 8mm print.
  • Below minimum, use the circle mark alone (favicon / app icon).
Do / Don't
  • Don't recolor the rings — they inherit the circle's ink only.
  • Don't stretch or rotate the lockup or alter word spacing.
  • Don't add effects — no shadows, gradients, or outlines.
