01 — The Reframe

What does it mean to thrive in college?

Colleges have always measured success. Thriving is bigger — students fully engaged intellectually, socially, and psychologically.

And unlike the things campuses can't change, every factor of thriving is malleable. Institutions can teach students to thrive.
