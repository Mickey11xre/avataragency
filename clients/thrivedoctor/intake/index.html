Your Project Intake A few questions so our kickoff meeting starts at full speed.

Welcome — and congratulations on getting started. This form is the same set of questions we'll walk through together in our creative kickoff meeting. Seeing them ahead of time gives you room to think, gather materials, and talk it over with anyone else involved in the decision.

Answer what you can. There are no wrong answers, and nothing here is locked in — it's a starting point for the conversation, not a contract. If a question doesn't apply to you, skip it. If you're not sure, say so; "I don't know yet" is genuinely useful information for us.

You don't have to finish this in one sitting. Your answers save automatically as you type, and there's a Save Progress button in the corner of the screen you can press anytime.

Coming back later? Just open this same link again — on your computer, tablet, or phone. Everything you've written will be exactly where you left it.
