About Harvard Investment Group, its investments, its developments and Hope for the Hurting.

What is Harvard Investment Group?

Harvard Investment Group is a multi-dimensional organization, founded in 1995 by Greg Cullen, that has been actively investing in and developing real estate for over three decades, along with its affiliated companies. It also makes direct investments in early stage businesses.

Who leads Harvard Investment Group?

Greg Cullen, the founder. He holds an MBA from Harvard Business School (1989) and a mechanical engineering degree from Rose-Hulman (1985), worked at Citibank and ING Capital before founding the company, and is a licensed contractor and real estate broker in California.

Where is Harvard Investment Group based?

The office is at 6060 W Manchester Ave, Suite 304, Los Angeles, CA 90045. Development projects have been in Corona del Mar (Newport Beach), the Hollywood Hills, Hermosa Beach and Redondo Beach.

What does Harvard Investment Group invest in?

Value-added real estate opportunities and early stage businesses that need capital and/or management expertise. Past investments span media, artificial intelligence, pharmaceutical, finance, medical devices, fast food, energy, real estate, entertainment and apparel.

How much does Harvard Investment Group typically invest?

Anywhere from the mid-five figures to seven figures, depending on the transaction. The goal is to help build a solid management team, a firm financial foundation and a strategy that leads to a profitable exit.

Does Harvard Investment Group take an active role in its investments?

Yes. Its principal often provides direction and corporate support at the board level, and has also assisted companies on an interim basis in executive and operational roles.

What kind of real estate does Harvard Investment Group develop?

It is a vertically integrated developer that buys land with unrealized development potential and underperforming real estate assets, then transforms them into for-sale product or assets held for cash flow. Work has ranged from home remodeling to full-scale land subdivision and project development.

What is the Corona del Mar development?

Two luxury estates built on the sands of Newport Harbor, each with roughly 10,000 square feet of livable area, a private beach and a private dock. 2201 Bayside Drive sold for $24,500,000 and 2209 Bayside Drive sold for $23,000,000.

Was a Harvard Investment Group project featured on Netflix?

Yes. The Corona del Mar project appears in Selling the OC, Season 3, on Netflix.

Who designed 2201 Bayside Drive?

Brandon Architects, with interiors by Brooke Wagner Design. The home has five bedrooms and nine bathrooms on four levels, a rooftop deck, wine room, elevator, outdoor fire pit, pool, jacuzzi and a 6-car garage.

What is Hope for the Hurting?

A 501(c)(3) charitable organization Mr. Cullen formed in 2015 to assist the less fortunate and provide a foundation for people to improve their lives. It operates four facilities in California and Indiana for men and women facing mental illness and/or addiction, and runs the Strike Out Against Drugs school program.

I’m a founder looking for funding. Is Harvard Investment Group a fit?

Possibly. Harvard Investment Group invests in early stage businesses that need capital and/or management expertise, typically from the mid-five figures to seven figures, depending on the transaction. It has invested in media, artificial intelligence, pharmaceuticals, finance, medical devices, fast food, energy, real estate, entertainment and apparel. Every opportunity is reviewed individually by Greg Cullen.

How do I pitch my company?

Send a short summary to Greg Cullen’s office through the contact page: the company name and what it does, the stage it is at, how much capital it is raising and what for, and the founder’s name, email and phone number. Every decision follows a review; nothing is promised in advance.

Can Harvard Investment Group help beyond money?

Yes. Its principal often supports companies at the board level and has stepped into interim executive and operational roles. The team brings expertise in deal structuring, valuation, financial engineering, corporate strategy and value creation.

Do you buy land or underperforming properties?

That is the company’s specialty: land with unrealized development potential and underperforming real estate assets, which it transforms into homes for sale or assets held for cash flow. A seller or broker with a property should share the address and details with Greg Cullen’s office through the contact page.

Does Harvard Investment Group take on development partners?

It works with partners on land, entitlements, construction and sales, and handles every part of the process itself: acquisition, deal structuring, finance, architectural design, entitlements, construction and real estate sales. Anyone with a site, a project or a partnership idea should send a short description through the contact page.

Are any of your homes for sale right now?

The homes on this site are completed projects, and the two Corona del Mar estates have sold. For current or upcoming opportunities, ask Greg Cullen’s office through the contact page.

How do I donate to Hope for the Hurting, and is it tax deductible?

Donate through Hope for the Hurting’s own website, hopefth.org. Hope for the Hurting is a 501(c)(3) charitable organization and all donations are tax deductible; confirm the details with your own tax advisor. Donations support its four facilities for men and women facing mental illness and/or addiction, and the Strike Out Against Drugs program in Los Angeles schools.

Can I donate property or other assets?

The charity has received property before: Oasis Recovery in Indiana was a donation from a generous couple. Anyone considering a gift like that should speak with Greg Cullen’s office.

Is Greg Cullen available to speak at events?

Yes. Mr. Cullen is a highly sought-after speaker and is considered an expert in real estate, finance, negotiations and value creation. Send speaking requests to his office through the contact page.
