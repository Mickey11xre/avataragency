Softlips “Cherry” — What Your Lips Say About You

10–15 second ultra-realistic concept film · 16:9 · Storyboard v2 · July 22, 2026
How to read this board: each frame below is a true production still, not an illustration — character identity is locked to the approved “Cherry Girl” reference imagery, so what you see is what the finished film will look like. Timing, camera movement, and transitions are described under each frame.

Voice & sound: the film is deliberately wordless through its middle — balm-glide foley and a minimal music bed carry it. Cherry’s voice (young South African female, per the accompanying voice sample) appears only as two bookends: a long whispered “Sssshhuuusshh…” opening the film, and a soft spoken “So… what do your lips say about you?” landing over the end card. The same voice powers the interactive Cherry avatar, so the film and the Companion share one sonic identity. © AvatarAgency — concept, licensed for pitch review only.
SHOT 1 · 00:00–00:02The Touch

Open in extreme macro, already intimate: the balm tip meets the center of her lower lip in deep slow motion (≈25% speed). The cherry core compresses ever so slightly; a glossy sheen blooms where it touches. Prismatic flare drifts through the top of frame. Camera: locked-off macro, breath-level micro-drift only. VO stem 1: Cherry’s long whispered “Sssshhuuusshh…” opens the film over this shot — her voice arrives before her face does.

SHOT 2 · 00:02–00:04.5Shusssh

Soft-bloom transition to the frontal hero composition: she holds the stick vertical to her lips — the campaign’s “shusssh” gesture — with direct, serene eye contact. Slow 8–10% push-in toward her eyes. The flare crosses behind her head; the tail of her whispered hush dissolves into the music bed.

SHOT 3 · 00:04.5–00:07The Glow

Cut on the flare to three-quarter angle: she applies at the corner of a beginning smile, gaze drifting dreamily off-frame. Gentle counter-clockwise orbital drift (≈10°) around her, rim light tracing loose hair strands. Everything moves like silk — slow, warm, unhurried.

SHOT 4 · 00:07–00:09The GlideSignature Shot

Hard cut into the film’s signature image: side-angle extreme macro as the balm glides across her upper lip in very deep slow motion (≈15–20% speed), leaving a wet, glistening cherry-sheen trail with micro-sparkle. Golden rim light from behind; focus racks with the moving tip. This is the shot that sells the product and the capability.

SHOT 5 · 00:09–00:11The Payoff

Speed ramps back to natural: medium close-up, chin dips, and she gives the camera a closed-lip knowing smile — lips now carrying the finished sheer cherry gloss. The stick lowers just out of frame. This is “what your lips say about you” embodied: she doesn’t say a word.

SHOT 6 · 00:11–00:13Product Hero

Soft-bloom transition to the product world: the Repair Cherry stick hero-lit on pink silk, two glossy cherries at its base, fine droplets catching light. Slow 5% push-in; a single droplet slides down a cherry. The color story of her lips and the product resolve into one image.

SHOT 7 · 00:13–00:15End Card

Gentle crossfade to the campaign end card: “What your lips say about you” in berry serif, softlips wordmark beneath, retail line — Available on Amazon.co.za · Takealot.com. Type breathes in with a slow 2% scale; flare settles. All motion resolves to stillness. VO stem 2: Cherry’s voice, soft and unhurried, lands over the card — “So… what do your lips say about you?” — the only spoken line in the film.
