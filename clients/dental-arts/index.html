Questions Patients Ask

Straight answers about Dental Arts San Diego.

Dr. Alena Nawrocki, DDS · 707 Arnele Ave, El Cajon, CA 92020 · (619) 444-1001

Does Dental Arts San Diego have a Russian-speaking dentist?

Yes. Dr. Alena Nawrocki and members of her team speak Russian with patients every day at Dental Arts San Diego in El Cajon, California. Ask for Russian when you call (619) 444-1001 and we will schedule you with a Russian-speaking team member — no interpreter needed.

What languages does the team at Dental Arts San Diego speak?

The team speaks five languages: English, Russian, Spanish, Tagalog, and Japanese. Tell us which language you prefer when you book and we will match you with a team member who speaks it.

I am nervous about the dentist. How do you handle anxious patients?

Dental Arts San Diego is a judgment-free practice — no lectures about how long it has been since your last visit. Tell us you are nervous when you book, and we will explain each step before we begin, work at your pace, and stop whenever you ask.

Do you offer Invisalign in El Cajon?

Yes. Dental Arts San Diego provides Invisalign clear aligners and also offers traditional braces in-house, so you can compare both options with one doctor. Invisalign consultations are free, and a TRIOS digital scan can preview your finished smile before you commit.

Do you treat sleep apnea or snoring?

Yes. Dental Arts San Diego makes custom-fitted oral appliances for snoring and sleep apnea. Call (619) 444-1001 to ask about a sleep appliance evaluation.

Do you remove wisdom teeth?

Yes. Wisdom tooth evaluation and removal are performed in-house at Dental Arts San Diego in El Cajon, using 3D CBCT imaging to assess root position before treatment.

Do you treat gum disease and perform bone grafting?

Yes. Dental Arts San Diego provides periodontal (gum) treatment and surgery, including bone grafting to rebuild bone before or alongside dental implants.

Is Dental Arts San Diego accepting new patients?

Yes, Dental Arts San Diego is welcoming new patients. New patients can start with a flat $125 package that includes a comprehensive exam, necessary X-rays, and a consultation with Dr. Nawrocki. Implant, Invisalign, and veneer consultations are free.

Are you a family dentist? Do you see children?

Yes. Dental Arts San Diego is a family practice caring for children, adults, and seniors, and families often book their visits together. Dr. Nawrocki has served El Cajon families for more than 25 years.

Can I get implants, Invisalign, and veneers at the same practice?

Yes. Cleanings and exams, teeth whitening, Invisalign, braces, porcelain veneers, crowns, bridges, root canals, wisdom teeth, periodontal surgery, and dental implants are all handled at Dental Arts San Diego — one doctor coordinates your entire treatment plan instead of referring you across town.

Can I be seen the same day for a dental emergency?

Yes. Dental Arts San Diego keeps same-day appointments open for dental emergencies. Call (619) 444-1001 as early in the day as you can.

Are you open evenings or on Saturdays?

Yes. Dental Arts San Diego offers Tuesday evening appointments and Saturday hours for patients who cannot take time off work. Call (619) 444-1001 for this week’s schedule.

What is All-on-4 and do you offer it?

All-on-4 replaces a full arch of missing teeth with a fixed bridge supported by four dental implants, so it stays in place without the adhesive a removable denture needs. Dental Arts San Diego provides All-on-4 full-arch implant restoration in El Cajon.

Am I too old for dental implants?

Age alone rarely rules out dental implants. What matters is bone volume and general health, which Dr. Nawrocki evaluates with a 3D CBCT scan at your free implant consultation.

Do you take dental insurance, and what does a visit cost?

Dental Arts San Diego accepts PPO and HMO dental plans and offers payment plans. New patients can begin with a flat $125 exam, X-ray, and consultation package, and implant, Invisalign, and veneer consultations are free.

Where is Dental Arts San Diego located and which areas do you serve?

Dental Arts San Diego is at 707 Arnele Ave, El Cajon, CA 92020, serving El Cajon and East County San Diego — including La Mesa, Santee, Lakeside, Spring Valley, and Alpine.
