Executive Summary

The opportunity

A subscription that puts a cloned, interactive AI digital twin or custom avatar spokesperson on every real-estate agent's website — talking to visitors 24/7, capturing leads, and (in higher tiers) producing cinematic AI video. Built on the Napster Companion API (Microsoft Azure-native), whose usage economics let us price an entry tier at under $1/day while staying profitable.

Three tiers — Spokesperson / Studio / Signature ($29 / $149 / $399 per month, billed annually) — with a built-in, zero-extra-cost differentiator: "Speed-to-Lead" real-time lead alerts. The strategic goal is volume: land many agent twins while the Napster window is open, then expand margin through video-production automation.

Bottom line: low entry price, healthy margins on annual billing, and a lead-gen story that justifies the subscription and reduces churn.
Section 1

The Program & Features

A monthly/annual subscription program. Every plan includes the interactive avatar and lead tools; higher tiers add cinematic video.

1 · Interactive Avatar / Digital Twin or Custom Avatar Spokes Person all tiers

A photoreal digital twin of the agent — their own face and voice — or a professional custom avatar spokesperson (like our "Ava") for agents who prefer not to appear on camera. Embedded on the agent's website via the Napster Companion API, it greets every visitor, answers questions about listings and the buying/selling process, and captures lead details, 24/7. The cloning/build session is included (annual plans). This is the core of every tier.

2 · Cinematic AI Video Studio & Signature

30-second cinematic videos produced from the agent's photos/clips via our Fal.ai pipeline — branded intro films, property tours, listing reels — delivered in horizontal (YouTube) or vertical (Reels / TikTok / Shorts) format, ready for web and social. Studio includes 1/month; Signature up to 4/month. Replaces $3,000–$7,000 traditional production per the AvatarAgency case study.

3 · "Speed-to-Lead" Alerts + Conversation Inbox all tiers

The instant a visitor finishes a chat, the agent gets a real-time SMS/email — who they are, what they want, and their contact info — plus a searchable inbox of every conversation. In real estate, first-to-call-back wins; this turns the avatar from a novelty into a lead-gen machine and is the single biggest retention driver. Built on Napster Webhooks + Sessions API at no added platform cost.

Why it wins

Record once, show up everywhere. One build session becomes an always-on website salesperson plus a content engine — at a price point no traditional crew or per-avatar competitor can match, because Napster's usage-metered, Azure-native model collapses the cost of running a live avatar.

Optional Mini Stations available for real estate open houses and special events.

Pilot program & cloning session

This is a pilot program currently offered only in the County of Los Angeles. Setup includes a Digital Cloning Session at our Woodland Hills studio. An optional Onsite Cloning Session at your location is available for an additional $99 travel fee on the Spokesperson and Studio plans — and is free for Signature annual subscriptions.

On the $0.05/min chat cost (validated)

gpt-realtime token rates: audio $32/1M in, $64/1M out (cached in $0.40/1M). At 600 user + 1,200 assistant tokens per minute of speech, the realistic conversational cost with prompt caching lands ~$0.05–0.10/min. Our actual Azure bill is $0.05/min all-in — i.e. Napster already does the caching + committed-use optimization for us. Same-quality "bring-your-own-LLM" would be a wash, so we start on Hosted. (A cheaper model is the only real BYO saving — a future quality test.)

Video is the cost to attack

At ~$50/finished 30s video, video is our only meaningful variable cost and it caps the video tiers' margin. Initiative: automate the 30s pipeline + source cheaper rendering (with Fal.ai). Every $1 shaved off per-video flows straight to Studio/Signature profit (≈ $1,200/yr per Signature client at 4 videos/mo for each $25 saved).

Billing, breakeven & churn

  • Annual billing is cash-positive on day one — the upfront payment covers the build, COGS, and acquisition immediately, and neutralizes churn risk. This is why annual is the hero.
  • Monthly breaks even ~month 3; the $99 Spokesperson setup fee covers the build so an early cancel only costs acquisition.
  • CAC must stay low (organic/referral, ~$50) — at $29 there's no room for expensive paid acquisition. Self-serve onboarding keeps delivery cost ≤ $100.

⚠ Margin sensitivity — Signature

Signature's margin rides entirely on per-video cost: $50/video → ~$153/mo; $80/video → ~$33/mo. Confirm real per-video COGS before scaling Signature; the video-automation initiative is the protection. If per-video stays ≥ $70, raise Signature to ~$449 or cap at 3 videos.

Overage & minutes

Overage is $0.20/min — a 4× markup on the $0.05 cost, so heavy users remain profitable and the cap removes "what happens at minute 101?" anxiety. Included minutes (100 / 300 / 600) are framed as a benefit ("~40 conversations included"), not a meter.

Disclaimers & Founding-Member pricing

Current rates are positioned as Founding-Member / introductory pricing — creating urgency, rewarding early adopters with a locked rate, and reserving the right to adjust pricing for future cohorts as costs are confirmed and video automation lands.

Recommended small print (under the public pricing grid):
"Founding-member introductory pricing — rates may change. Your price is locked for your current billing term; any change takes effect at renewal with at least 30 days' notice. Chat overage billed at $0.20/min. Monthly video allotments don't roll over. Build session included on annual plans; a one-time $99 setup applies to the monthly Spokesperson plan. Videos are AI-generated and subject to our content & fair-use guidelines."

Back the disclaimer with a real Terms of Service page; a brief legal review is recommended before launch. AI-generated synthetic-performer disclosure already appears site-wide.

Section 5

Roadmap & Open Items

Build initiatives

  • Speed-to-Lead layer — webhook receiver (Cloudflare Pages Function) + a capture_lead Tool + Sessions API → real-time SMS/email alerts + a multi-tenant per-agent conversation inbox/dashboard.
  • Video-production automation — cheaper render sources + a templated 30s pipeline (with Fal.ai) to push per-video COGS below $50 and expand Studio/Signature margin.

Open items to confirm before launch

  • Actual fully-loaded cost per finished 30s video (drives the Signature margin).
  • Napster webhook payload schema (inspect a live event) and whether capture_lead tool results appear in the session/webhook.
  • Transcript retention period (undocumented) — don't promise "history forever" until confirmed.
  • Whether to gate SMS alerts / analytics depth by tier, or include for all.
  • Terms of Service + data/PII handling for stored lead transcripts.
