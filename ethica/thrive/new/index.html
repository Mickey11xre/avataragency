What is the PDR Framework for fighting fraud?

PDR stands for Prevention, Detection and Remediation. Prevention puts policies, password controls, approved vendor lists and internal controls in place before fraud can start. Detection uses forensic accounting, data analysis, audit trails and anomaly detection to spot fraud in progress. Remediation restores financial integrity, recovers losses, reports to the right authorities and educates victims.

How can a small business prevent fraud?

Start with controls any team can put in place: require dual authorization for large transactions, separate financial duties so no one person controls a payment from end to end, reconcile bank statements every month, keep an approved vendor list, run surprise point-of-sale audits, and train staff to recognize phishing and social-engineering attempts.

What should I do if I think I'm a victim of fraud?

Act quickly. Contact your bank or card issuer to freeze accounts and dispute charges, change your passwords, and keep records of everything. In the United States, report fraud at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and identity theft at IdentityTheft.gov. Ethica can walk you through what each step involves and what to expect.
