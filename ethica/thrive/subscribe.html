Frequently asked questions

What counts as a "minute"?

Active conversation time with Ethica. Minutes only count while you're in a live session — not while the page is open idle.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings at any time and you won't be charged after your current billing period ends.

Do unused minutes roll over?

Not currently. Each billing cycle starts fresh. We're exploring rollover options for a future update.

Is Ethica a real lawyer or accountant?

No — Ethica is an AI avatar for educational purposes only. Her guidance is not legal or financial advice. Always consult a licensed professional for your specific situation.

What payment methods are accepted?

All major credit and debit cards via Stripe. Apple Pay and Google Pay are also supported at checkout.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, switch between Starter, Guardian, and Defender anytime. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
