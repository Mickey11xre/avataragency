Store

Buy directly

Every product, priced as it is charged. Choose a quantity and buy on Stripe's secure page by card or US bank debit. Ordering for more than one population, or three or more survey packages? Build a combined order and the 15% discount is applied automatically. Institutions buying on a purchase order can request an invoice instead.

Survey packages

One per population you are surveying. You choose the instrument on the payment page.

  • Survey Package

    $750 per survey

    Survey only. We set up the survey and send you the link; the raw data is returned when the survey closes. No national-norms report and no interactive dashboard.

  • Basic Report Package

    $1,500 per survey

    Survey setup, the link and the raw data, plus a report comparing your institution’s data to national norms once norms are established for the semester, plus an interactive dashboard for disaggregation by gender and race/ethnicity.

  • Expanded Report Package Most complete

    $2,500 per survey

    We administer the survey from an email list you provide, with up to 10 institution-specific questions included. You receive the raw data, the norms-comparison report, the interactive dashboard, and a predictive model specific to your campus — conditional on more than 300 responses.

Campus package

  • Thriving Campus Package with One-Hour Webinar

    $10,000

    Any five instruments administered within a one-year period, with up to 10 institution-specific questions per survey. Raw data, a norms-comparison report for each of the five, a predictive model per survey (with more than 300 responses) and interactive dashboards. Plus a one-hour webinar with Dr. Schreiner: results, recommended actions and national trends, synthesised across all five surveys with a thematic analysis of comments. Unlimited stakeholders, recordable.

  • Additional Demographic Variable

  • One-Hour Webinar with Dr. Laurie Schreiner
