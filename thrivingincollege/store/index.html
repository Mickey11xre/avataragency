Store

Buy directly

Every product, priced as it is charged. Choose a quantity and buy on Stripe's secure page by card or US bank debit. Ordering for more than one population, or three or more survey packages? Build a combined order and the 15% discount is applied automatically. Institutions buying on a purchase order can request an invoice instead.

Survey packages

One per population you are surveying. You choose the instrument on the payment page.

  • Survey Package

    $750 per survey

    Survey administration only. The instrument is administered to your campus and the results are returned without a report.

  • Basic Report Package

    $1,500 per survey

    Survey administration plus a report of your own institution's results.

  • Expanded Report Package Most complete

    $2,500 per survey

    Survey administration plus comparative reporting against other institutions. The predictive model is included when your campus returns more than 300 responses.

Campus package

  • Thriving Campus Package with One-Hour Webinar

    $10,000

    The full campus package, including a one-hour webinar with Dr. Schreiner. Priced as a whole; the multi-survey discount does not apply. Business offices can also request a Net-30 invoice.

Add-ons and webinar

Add-ons attach to a survey or report package you have ordered.

  • Additional Institution-Specific Questions

    $300 per group of five

    Five questions of your own added to the survey. Quantity is the number of groups of five.

  • Additional Demographic Variable

    $300 per variable

    An extra demographic variable in the interactive dashboard of a report package.

  • One-Hour Webinar with Dr. Laurie Schreiner

    $2,000

    A standalone one-hour webinar for your campus.

Researcher access — $250

Permission to use a Thriving Quotient instrument in a single dissertation or study. This is not sold here: submit your proposal first, and once Dr. Schreiner returns the signed proposal you receive a payment link and the instrument.
