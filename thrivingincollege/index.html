01 — The Reframe

What does it mean to thrive in college?

Colleges have always measured success. Thriving is bigger: students fully engaged intellectually, socially, and psychologically.

And unlike the things campuses can't change, every factor of thriving is malleable. Institutions can teach students to thrive.

02 — The Researcher

Who created the Thriving Quotient?

Dr. Laurie A. Schreiner, Professor Emerita of Higher Education at Azusa Pacific University, co-editor-in-chief of Christian Higher Education and co-editor of Thriving in Transitions. She introduced the Thriving Quotient in 2010 and has spent forty-five years asking a better question about student success.

04 — The Evidence

What has fifteen years of research shown?

The Thriving Quotient rests on peer-reviewed publications, conference papers and doctoral dissertations that tested the model with specific populations. It is all in the Thriving Knowledge Center, so a research office can check the instrument before a campus buys it.
