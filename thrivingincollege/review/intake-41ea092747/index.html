Try it with different orders

The form changes to match what was bought. These are made-up example orders, not real purchases. Nothing you type here is sent anywhere, so feel free to fill it in and press "Send" at the bottom to see the summary email you would receive.

How it will work

  1. A campus pays in the store.
  2. Stripe sends them straight to this form, already filled in with their order: package, instrument, institution and PO number.
  3. When they press Send, you get one email with everything you need to set up the survey, plus a short list of anything still missing.
  4. They get a copy and a private link to come back and finish, so their business office or institutional research office can complete their parts.
  5. Campuses that pay by purchase order get the same form once your invoice is paid.
  6. If a form isn't finished within three days, one reminder goes out automatically.

What we need from you

  1. What else do you ask campuses today that isn't on this form?
  2. Student IDs: what consent wording do you use when IDs are collected, and when they are not?
  3. For surveys you administer: which columns should the email list have, and how do you want campuses to send it?
  4. How much lead time do you need between receiving this form and launching a survey?
  5. What is your standard reminder schedule?
  6. Which demographic breakdowns are in every dashboard by default? We assumed gender and race/ethnicity.
  7. Should the completed form go only to you, or to you and info@thrivingincollege.org?
