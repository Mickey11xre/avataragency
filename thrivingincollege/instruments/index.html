Instruments

Seven instruments, one per campus population

The Thriving Quotient is a family of validated survey instruments, each built for a different population on campus. All measure thriving on scales an institution can act on, all are administered the same way, and all are priced the same: survey administration from $750, with report packages from $1,500. Choose by who you are surveying.

For students

  • Undergraduate Thriving Quotient

    The core instrument. Five scales of undergraduate thriving — engaged learning, academic determination, positive perspective, diverse citizenship and social connectedness.

    Undergraduate

  • Sophomore Experiences Survey

    For second-year students, at the point in the degree where institutions most often lose them.

    Sophomore

  • Adult Thriving Quotient

    For adult learners — students who are also working, parenting, or returning to study.

    Adult Learner

  • Graduate Student Thriving Quotient

    For master's and doctoral students.

    Graduate

  • Community College Thriving Quotient

    For students in the two-year setting, where the student body and its pressures differ from a four-year campus.

    Community College

For faculty and staff

  • Faculty Thriving Quotient

    For faculty members. An adjunct faculty variant is chosen as the instrument at checkout.

    Faculty

  • Staff Thriving Quotient

    For professional and administrative staff.

    Staff

Choosing an instrument

Which Thriving Quotient instrument should our campus use?

Choose by population. There is one instrument each for undergraduates, second-year students, adult learners, graduate students, community college students, faculty and staff. A campus surveying more than one population orders one survey package per population, and three or more earn a 15% discount.

Are the instruments administered differently?

No. Every instrument is administered the same way: your campus distributes a survey link to the sampled population, responses return directly to The Thriving Project, and reporting follows once the survey window closes.

Is there an adjunct faculty instrument?

Yes, as a variant of the Faculty Thriving Quotient. It is chosen as the instrument at checkout rather than sold as a separate product.
