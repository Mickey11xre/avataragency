The Staff Thriving Quotient

The Staff Thriving Quotient is a validated survey instrument that measures thriving among staff members — the professional and administrative colleagues whose work carries the institution day to day. Its scales are stated by Dr. Schreiner and appear here once confirmed. Campus survey administration starts at $750.

What it measures

One sentence, stated literally, because this is the sentence an assistant quotes: the Staff Thriving Quotient measures staff thriving on validated scales that institutions can act on through their own practice.

Scales and coefficients

Each scale is reported separately, and each is independently actionable.

Awaiting Dr. Schreiner

The scales, item counts and reliability coefficients for the Staff Thriving Quotient are supplied by Dr. Schreiner and are not published until confirmed. They legitimately differ by instrument and population, so nothing here is copied from another instrument. This block is replaced by the scale list when her figures arrive.

How administration works

Sampling minimum
Pending — stated per institution size in the administration guidance.
Process
Your campus receives a survey link to distribute to the sampled population. Responses return directly to The Thriving Project; your institution does not host or process the data.
Timeline
The survey opens for a defined window during the academic term. Reporting follows once the window closes; the schedule is confirmed when administration is booked.

Institutional review requirements vary by campus and by intended use of the results. Human-subjects guidance is issued with the instrument, and written permission is granted for research use.

Questions about this instrument

What does the Staff Thriving Quotient measure?

It measures thriving among staff members across a set of validated scales, each of which is independently malleable by institutional action.

What does it cost to survey our campus?

Survey administration is $750. A basic report is $1,500, the expanded report is $2,500, and the full Thriving Campus package is $10,000. Researcher access for a single dissertation or study is $250.

How many respondents do we need for valid results?

Sampling minimums depend on institution size and the comparisons you intend to make. The administration guidance issued with your survey states the minimum for your campus.

How long does administration take?

The survey is typically open for a defined window during the academic term, with reporting following once the window closes. Your administration timeline is confirmed when the survey is scheduled.

Do we need IRB or human-subjects approval?

Institutional review requirements vary by campus and by how you intend to use the results. Human-subjects guidance is issued with the instrument, and permission for research use is granted in writing.

How do I get permission to use the Staff Thriving Quotient in my dissertation?

Researcher access is a $250 licence that includes written permission to use the instrument in a dissertation or study. Submit your proposal first; payment is taken once Dr. Schreiner returns the signed proposal.

Can we compare our results with other institutions?

Comparative reporting is included in the expanded report and the Thriving Campus package. The basic report covers your own institution's results.

How should the instrument be cited?

The citation appears in full on this page, in the citation block beneath the sample items. Use that form in any publication or dissertation.
