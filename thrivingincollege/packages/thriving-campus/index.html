Thriving Campus Package with One-Hour Webinar

$10,000. Any five instruments administered within a one-year period, with up to 10 institution-specific questions per survey. Raw data, a norms-comparison report for each of the five, a predictive model per survey (with more than 300 responses) and interactive dashboards. Plus a one-hour webinar with Dr. Schreiner: results, recommended actions and national trends, synthesised across all five surveys with a thematic analysis of comments. Unlimited stakeholders, recordable.

What is included

  • Any five instruments administered within a one-year period.
  • Up to ten institution-specific questions per survey.
  • Raw data, a norms-comparison report for each of the five, interactive dashboards.
  • A predictive model per survey, when more than 300 responses are returned.
  • A one-hour webinar with Dr. Schreiner: results, recommended actions and national trends, synthesised across all five surveys with a thematic analysis of comments. Unlimited stakeholders, recordable.

Who it is for

Institutions taking a whole-campus view of thriving across students, faculty and staff in one year, with Dr. Schreiner's own reading of the results for the leadership.

Questions about this package

Which five instruments can we choose?

Any five of the Thriving Quotient instruments, administered within a one-year period: undergraduate, sophomore, adult learner, graduate, community college, faculty, adjunct faculty or staff.

Does the 15% discount apply to the Thriving Campus Package?

No. The package is priced as a whole. The 15% discount applies only to three or more survey packages bought individually.

Can our institution pay by purchase order?

Yes. Request a quote and a Net-30 invoice, payable by ACH bank transfer or card. Checkout by card or bank debit is also available.
