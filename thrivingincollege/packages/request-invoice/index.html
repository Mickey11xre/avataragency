Questions about invoicing

Can our institution pay by purchase order?

Yes. Request a quote or invoice with this form, add your purchase order number now or later, and pay the hosted invoice by ACH bank transfer or card on Net-30 terms.

Is a W-9 available?

Yes. Tick the W-9 box on the request form and it is sent with the quote.

Does the multi-survey discount apply to invoiced orders?

Yes. An order of three or more survey packages — Survey, Basic Report or Expanded Report in any combination — receives 15% off those packages whether it is paid at checkout or by invoice. The discount never applies to the Thriving Campus package, add-ons or the standalone webinar.
