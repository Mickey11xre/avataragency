Institutional purchasing

Your request has been received

Dr. Schreiner's office will reply by email with a quote and a reference number for your budget packet. When you are ready, the Net-30 invoice can be paid by ACH bank transfer or card. Nothing has been charged.

What happens next
A quote arrives by email, usually within a few business days. Reply to it with your purchase order number if you did not include one.
Paying
The invoice carries a secure payment link. ACH bank transfer clears in four to five business days; card payments confirm immediately.
Then
Once payment is confirmed, survey administration is scheduled with your campus.
