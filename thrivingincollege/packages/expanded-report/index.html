Expanded Report Package

$2,500 per survey. We administer the survey from an email list you provide, with up to 10 institution-specific questions included. You receive the raw data, the norms-comparison report, the interactive dashboard, and a predictive model specific to your campus — conditional on more than 300 responses.

What is included

  • The Thriving Project administers the survey from an email list you provide.
  • Up to ten institution-specific questions, included.
  • The raw data, the national-norms comparison report and the interactive dashboard.
  • A predictive model of thriving specific to your campus, when more than 300 responses are returned.

Stated up front, at Dr. Schreiner's instruction

The predictive model depends on sample size. A campus returning fewer than 300 responses receives everything else in this package but not the model.

Who it is for

Campuses that want the fullest picture of a single population: benchmarked results, their own questions, and a model of what predicts thriving on their campus specifically.

Questions about this package

What is the predictive model?

A model of thriving specific to your campus, built from your own responses. It is included when your survey returns more than 300 responses; below that threshold the sample cannot support it.

Are institution-specific questions included?

Yes, up to ten are included in the Expanded Report Package. Further questions are $300 per group of five.

Who administers the survey?

The Thriving Project administers it from an email list you provide, rather than your campus distributing a link.
