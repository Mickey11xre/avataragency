Basic Report Package

$1,500 per survey. Survey setup, the link and the raw data, plus a report comparing your institution’s data to national norms once norms are established for the semester, plus an interactive dashboard for disaggregation by gender and race/ethnicity.

What is included

  • Survey setup, the survey link, and the raw data when the survey closes.
  • A report comparing your institutional data to national norms, once norms are established for the semester.
  • An interactive dashboard for disaggregation by gender and race/ethnicity.

Who it is for

Campuses that want to see where they stand against national norms and explore their own results by group, without a custom model.

Add-ons and discount

Institution-specific questions
$300 per group of five, added to your survey.
Demographic variables
$300 per additional variable in the interactive dashboard.
Multi-survey discount
Order three or more survey packages in any combination and 15% comes off those packages automatically at checkout.

Questions about this package

What is in the Basic Report?

A comparison of your institution's data to national norms, once norms are established for the semester, plus an interactive dashboard that disaggregates results by gender and race/ethnicity. You also receive the raw data.

Can we add demographic variables to the dashboard?

Yes. Additional demographic variables in the interactive dashboard are $300 per variable.

How does it differ from the Expanded Report Package?

The Expanded Report Package adds up to ten institution-specific questions, has The Thriving Project administer the survey from your email list, and includes a predictive model specific to your campus when more than 300 responses are returned.
