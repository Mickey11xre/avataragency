Survey Package

$750 per survey. Survey only. We set up the survey and send you the link; the raw data is returned when the survey closes. No national-norms report and no interactive dashboard.

What is included

  • Survey setup for one instrument and one population, by The Thriving Project team.
  • A survey link for your campus to distribute to the sampled population.
  • The raw data, returned when the survey closes.

Not included: a national-norms comparison report or an interactive dashboard. If you want either, choose the Basic Report Package or the Expanded Report Package.

Who it is for

Institutions with their own research capacity that want the validated instrument administered cleanly and the data returned to analyse in-house.

Questions about this package

What do we receive with the Survey Package?

Survey setup, a survey link to distribute to your sampled population, and the raw data returned when the survey closes. It does not include a national-norms report or an interactive dashboard; those come with the Basic Report and Expanded Report packages.

Can we add our own questions to the Survey Package?

Yes. Additional institution-specific questions are $300 per group of five.

Does the Survey Package count toward the 15% discount?

Yes. Any three or more survey packages in one order, in any combination of Survey, Basic Report and Expanded Report, receive 15% off those packages at checkout.
