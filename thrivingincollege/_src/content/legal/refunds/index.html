Effective 18 September 2026.

This policy covers survey packages, report packages, add-ons, webinars and the researcher license purchased from The Thriving Project, whether paid by card, by ACH bank transfer or against an invoice.

Survey and report packages

  • Canceled before survey administration is scheduled: full refund, less a 5% cancellation fee.
  • Canceled after administration is scheduled but before data collection begins: full refund, less a 5% cancellation fee.
  • Canceled after data collection has started: no refund.
  • Additional institution-specific questions and additional demographic variables follow the policy of the survey they are added to.
  • For the Thriving Campus Package, these terms apply to each of its surveys separately; its webinar follows the webinar terms below.

ACH bank payments

An ACH payment canceled while it is still clearing is refunded, less a 5% cancellation fee.

Webinars

You are welcome to reschedule a webinar. If you cancel instead:

  • 30 days or more before the scheduled date: 90% refund.
  • 15 to 29 days before: 50% refund.
  • Fewer than 15 days before: no refund.

Researcher license

The {{price:researcher}} researcher license is non-refundable.

How to request a refund

Refund and cancellation requests must be made in writing, by email to info@thrivingincollege.org. Please include your institution, your order or invoice number, and what you are canceling. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.
