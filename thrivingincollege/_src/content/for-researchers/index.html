How it works

  1. Submit your proposal. Download the researcher request form, complete it with your study design and the instrument you need, and send it to Dr. Schreiner.
  2. Receive the signed permission. Dr. Schreiner reviews the proposal and returns it signed. Written permission is what your IRB and your committee will ask for.
  3. Pay the researcher licence. A {{price:researcher}} payment link is sent with the signed proposal.
  4. Receive the instrument. A copy of the instrument you requested is released on payment.

Questions from researchers

How do I get permission to use the Thriving Quotient in my dissertation?

Submit your proposal using the researcher request form. When Dr. Schreiner returns the signed proposal, you pay the {{price:researcher}} researcher licence and receive a copy of the instrument you requested, with written permission to use it.

What does researcher access cost?

{{price:researcher}} for a single dissertation or study, paid after the proposal is approved.

Which instruments can I request?

Any of the Thriving Quotient instruments: undergraduate, sophomore, adult learner, graduate, community college, faculty, adjunct faculty or staff.
