Questions about this package

Which five instruments can we choose?

Any five of the Thriving Quotient instruments, administered within a one-year period: undergraduate, sophomore, adult learner, graduate, community college, faculty, adjunct faculty or staff.

Does the 15% discount apply to the Thriving Campus Package?

No. The package is priced as a whole. The 15% discount applies only to three or more survey packages bought individually.

Yes. Request a quote and a Net-30 invoice, payable by ACH bank transfer or card. Checkout by card or bank debit is also available.
