Questions about this package

What is the predictive model?

A model of thriving specific to your campus, built from your own responses. It is included when your survey returns more than 300 responses; below that threshold the sample cannot support it.

Are institution-specific questions included?

Yes, up to ten are included in the Expanded Report Package. Further questions are {{price:addon-questions}} per group of five.

Who administers the survey?

The Thriving Project administers it from an email list you provide, rather than your campus distributing a link.
