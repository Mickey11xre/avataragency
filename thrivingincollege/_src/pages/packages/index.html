Questions about pricing

What does it cost to survey our campus?

Survey administration is {{price:survey}}. A basic report is {{price:basic-report}}, the expanded report is {{price:expanded-report}}, and the Thriving Campus package with a one-hour webinar is {{price:thriving-campus}}. Researcher access for a single dissertation or study is {{price:researcher}}.

Is there a discount for surveying more than one population?

Yes. When an order includes three or more survey packages — Survey, Basic Report or Expanded Report, in any combination — 15% comes off those packages automatically at checkout. The discount does not apply to the Thriving Campus package, add-ons or the standalone webinar.

Can we pay by purchase order or bank transfer?

Yes. Checkout accepts cards and US bank debit (ACH), and institutional buyers can request an invoice for payment by purchase order. Bank debit payments take four to five business days to clear; the order is confirmed once funds settle.

What is included in the expanded report?

Comparative reporting against other institutions in addition to your own results. The predictive model is included when your campus returns more than 300 responses.
