The research behind the instruments

Every Thriving Quotient instrument rests on published, peer-reviewed research into what it means for a student to thrive in college — and on the doctoral studies that tested that model with specific populations. This is the record: the publications, the conference papers, the dissertations, the recordings and the articles, kept here so that an institutional research office can check the instrument against the literature before it buys.

What thriving means here

Thriving is a fuller measure of student success than persistence or grades alone: the degree to which a student is engaged academically, connected interpersonally, and psychologically well. Each factor the Thriving Quotient measures is malleable — an institution can move it through its own practice — which is what makes the results actionable rather than merely descriptive.

The instruments built on this research are described on the Instruments pages; permission to use one in your own study is granted through Researcher access.
